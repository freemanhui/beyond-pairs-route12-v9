# Published scalar results

These CSVs contain simulated-cohort identities, scalar statistics, conservative add-one rank numerators and decisions; they contain no patient rows, genotype tables, fitted biological laws or executable model parameters. Purpose 0 is calibration; purpose 1 is evaluation. Law 0 is the specified pairwise null, law 7 the frozen alternative in confirmation/pools. Development laws 1,2,3,4,5,7 are the five reachable plants and frozen law in manuscript order.

`lrt.csv`, `sensitivity.csv` and `matched-oracle.csv` are long tables. Calibration rows have blank decision/numerator fields. `pools.csv` identifies all 40 separate calibration pools. Full precision scalar statistics permit exact rank-decision replay. Paired comparisons share cohort identities, not only aggregate counts.

`triple-coefficients-rounded.csv` transcribes the previously printed 120 coefficient summaries: natural parameter to four decimals and between-cohort SD to three. It does not supply singles/pairs, a probability vector, or unrounded fitted model. `hidden-coordinates.csv` contains signed information summaries, not hidden weights or component laws. Family covariance diagnostics and the five-point prediction sweep support the sandwich tables/Figure 1.

The full-mask/oracle-residual/learned-weight factorial method retains the numerical-sensitivity flag and all its results; it is not silently excluded. The positive and negative findings remain.

Reaggregation verifies reported arithmetic and decisions. It cannot independently validate missing model inputs, seed-to-count generation, fitting, or clinical validity. Data/provider conditions do not become permissions for excluded biological assets through this release. Cite the paper when using these reported summaries.
